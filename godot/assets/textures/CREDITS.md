No photo source and no third-party data went into these files. They are
placeholders with real structure. CC0 photo textures (e.g. ambientCG, Poly Haven,
1K/2K with albedo, normal and roughness) can replace single layers later: pack them
into the same strip layout and channels, then add a row per source here.

# Geodata of `dune_coast` (prompt 114)

Sources of the map `data/maps/dune_coast/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo dune_coast`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows Kampen on the island of Sylt
(Schleswig-Holstein, Germany): the North Sea beach and the Rotes Kliff in
the west, the dunes with the Uwe-Düne, the heath and the village in the
middle, the tidal flats of the Wadden Sea in the east, the
north edge of Wenningstedt in the south. The place is named only here, in
docs/SOURCES.md and in docs/MAPS.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query
  (`osm_query`), the query for fortifications (`fortifications_query`; its
  answer is not part of the extract, the one bunker it found lies just
  outside the frame; the other works in `fortifications.works` are
  invented positions on dune crests, marked so in `source`), the DGM1
  tile names (`dgm_tiles`), their index and licence, and the retrieval
  dates.
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-23 09:05 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads (plus `historic`,
  `military`, `wetland` and `aeroway`, `sources.extra_tags`); names,
  addresses and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: the official terrain model DGM1 of Schleswig-Holstein
  (1 m, ETRS89 / UTM zone 32N, EPSG:25832, 28 tiles of 1 km × 1 km as
  `x y z` text, survey dates 2005 and 2024 per tile), reduced to 2 m
  cells (mean of 2 × 2 pixels, centimeters) over the map frame plus
  100 m; cells farther away are empty. Source: Landesamt für Vermessung
  und Geoinformation Schleswig-Holstein, open geobasis data (OpenGBD),
  licence CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
  "© GeoBasis-DE/LVermGeo SH/CC BY 4.0 (Quelle verändert)" (data reduced
  and resampled).

Regenerate the extract (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the
repository; the DGM1 tiles from the links of the index GeoJSON
`sources.dgm_index_url`, one file per name in `sources.dgm_tiles`):

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/dune_coast/site.json \
        --osm build/geo114/raw/osm_raw.json \
        --dgm build/geo114/raw/dgm1/*.xyz

Comparison with the Copernicus DEM (tile N54 E008 from the AWS bucket
`copernicus-dem-30m`, only for the comparison, not part of the map):

    just gen-geo-compare dune_coast build/geo114/raw/cop_N54_E008.tif -486 build/geo114/stage1

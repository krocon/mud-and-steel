# Geodata of `landing_gulf` (prompt 121)

Sources of the map `data/maps/landing_gulf/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo landing_gulf`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows the Golfo di Campo on the south coast
of the island of Elba (Tuscany, Italy): Marina di Campo with its harbour
and the long sand beach, the plain of Campo behind it (today with the
airfield), the slopes of Monte Poro in the south-west and the hills
between the gulf and Punta di Fonza in the east. The mission of the map
follows the landing of Operation Brassard on 17 June 1944. The place is
named only here, in docs/SOURCES.md, docs/MAPS.md and docs/MISSIONS.md,
never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query
  (`osm_query`), the query for fortifications (`fortifications_query`; its
  answer is not part of the extract: OSM has no bunker in the frame, the
  places of the works are set by hand from the sources named in each
  `fortifications.works[].source`), the URL of the DEM tile and the
  retrieval dates.
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-23 17:49 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads (plus `historic`,
  `military`, `barrier`, `tourism` and `aeroway`, `sources.extra_tags`);
  names, addresses and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: crop of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tile N42 E010 in
  centimeters. Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014
  and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS
  by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

Regenerate the extract (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the
repository):

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/landing_gulf/site.json \
        --osm build/geo121/raw/osm_raw.json \
        --dem build/geo121/raw/cop_N42_E010.tif

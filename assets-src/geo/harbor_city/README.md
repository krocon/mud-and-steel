# Geodata of `harbor_city` (prompt 081)

Sources of the map `data/maps/harbor_city/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo harbor_city`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps from
real geodata"). The data shows Monaco and its neighbouring French communes;
the place is named only here and in docs/SOURCES.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query, the URLs and
  the retrieval dates of both sources.
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-17 18:25 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads; names, addresses and
  ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated map
  are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: crop of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tile N43 E007 in
  centimeters. Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014
  and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by
  the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

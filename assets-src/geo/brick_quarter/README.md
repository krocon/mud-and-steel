# Geodata of `brick_quarter` (prompt 112)

Sources of the map `data/maps/brick_quarter/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo brick_quarter`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows Manhattan (New York City) around
Greenwich Village: the West Village, the Village, SoHo and Hudson Square,
the south of Chelsea and the blocks east of Fifth Avenue, with the Hudson
waterfront. The place is named only here, in docs/SOURCES.md and in
docs/MAPS.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with both Overpass queries
  (`osm_query`, `osm_query_extra` for pier multipolygons), the URLs of the
  two DEM tiles and the retrieval dates.
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-23 06:14 and
  06:23 UTC), reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads; names,
  addresses and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: mosaic crop of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tiles N40 W074
  and N40 W075 in centimeters. Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR
  e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided
  under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

Regenerate the extract (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the
repository):

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/brick_quarter/site.json \
        --osm build/geo_raw_112/osm_overpass.json build/geo_raw_112/osm_extra.json \
        --dem build/geo_raw_112/copernicus_N40_00_W074_00.tif build/geo_raw_112/copernicus_N40_00_W075_00.tif

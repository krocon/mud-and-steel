# Geodata of `rhine_bridge` (prompt 118)

Sources of the map `data/maps/rhine_bridge/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo rhine_bridge`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows Arnhem (Gelderland, Netherlands) with
the Nederrijn and the road bridge of 1935 (today the John Frost bridge,
rebuilt on the same line), the old town and the quarters north of the
river and the polder of Malburgen in the south, set back to September
1944 where the data allow (docs/SOURCES.md). The place is named only here,
in docs/SOURCES.md, docs/MAPS.md and docs/MISSIONS.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query, the
  coverage service of the heights, the tile range and the retrieval
  dates, and the core (`core`: its polygon, the ground cleared for the
  bridge ramp of 1935, the rules for today's streets inside).
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-23 14:07 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads; names, addresses
  and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: AHN4 DTM (Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland, PDOK
  coverage service `dtm_05m`, CC0 1.0), fetched as 25 GeoTIFF tiles of
  1 km at 1 m in RD New (EPSG:28992), reduced to 2 m cells in
  centimeters with the gaps under buildings and water filled
  (format `geo_import dem rd v1`).
- `core_1944.json`: the core around the north end of the bridge as it
  was before the battle. `blocks`, `water` and `parks` (degrees × 1e7)
  were traced by `tools/geo_trace_core.py` from the Kadaster's
  topographic map of the Topotijdreis service (`Historische_tijdreis_1944`,
  level 11, an edition from before the bridge was built); `streets`,
  `buildings` (map meters) and `landmarks` were added by hand: the road
  down the north ramp, the streets and houses on both sides of the ramp,
  the great church of the old town (damaged). See `note` in the file.

Fetch again (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the repository): the
Overpass query of `site.json` once against `osm_endpoint`; the AHN tiles
with `sources.ahn_url` for x0 = 189000 … 193000 and y0 = 441000 …
445000 in steps of 1000 (x1 = x0 + 1000, y1 = y0 + 1000), then

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/rhine_bridge/site.json \
        --osm build/geo118/raw/osm_overpass.json --ahn build/geo118/raw/ahn/*.tif

The core is traced again (it keeps the hand-made parts of the file) from
the topographic tiles `t1944_<row>_<col>.jpg` of
`https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/nSZVuSZjHpEZZbRo/arcgis/rest/services/Historische_tijdreis_1944/MapServer/tile/11/<row>/<col>`
(rows 75460 … 75469, columns 75553 … 75562) with

    python3 tools/geo_trace_core.py assets-src/geo/rhine_bridge/site.json \
        build/geo118/raw/topo1944 assets-src/geo/rhine_bridge/core_1944.json --report build/geo118/trace

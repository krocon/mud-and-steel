# Geodata of `castle_rock` (prompt 127)

Sources of the map `data/maps/castle_rock/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo castle_rock`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows the inner city of Edinburgh
(Scotland): the Castle Rock with the castle, the Old Town, Princes Street
Gardens and Waverley station, the New Town, Calton Hill, Holyrood Park
with Arthur's Seat and Salisbury Crags, and the Water of Leith in the
north. The map shows a fictional fight around 1944; no battle was fought
there. The place is named only here, in docs/SOURCES.md and in
docs/MAPS.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query
  (`osm_query`), the URL of the DEM tile and the retrieval dates; the
  castle plateau height (`elevation.lifts`, 121 m; source: the rock's
  summit at 130 m) and the castle built from catalog objects
  (`landmarks`).
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-24 04:05 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads (plus `railway`,
  `historic`, `service` and `usage`, `sources.extra_tags`); names,
  addresses and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: crop of the Copernicus DEM GLO-30 tile N55 W004 in
  centimeters. Produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014
  and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS
  by the European Union and ESA; all rights reserved.

Regenerate the extract (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the
repository):

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/castle_rock/site.json \
        --osm build/geo127/raw/osm_raw.json \
        --dem build/geo127/raw/cop_N55_W004.tif

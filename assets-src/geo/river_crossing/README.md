# Geodata of `river_crossing` (prompt 116)

Sources of the map `data/maps/river_crossing/`, generated by
`tools/geo_import.py` (`just gen-geo river_crossing`, docs/MAPS.md, "Maps
from real geodata"). The data shows Nijmegen (Gelderland, Netherlands)
with the Waal and the Waal bridge, the village of Lent on the north bank
and the edge of the Ooijpolder, set back to the state of September 1944
where the data allow (docs/SOURCES.md). The place is named only here, in
docs/SOURCES.md, docs/MAPS.md and docs/MISSIONS.md, never in the game.

- `site.json`: the site description, with the Overpass query, the
  coverage service of the heights, the tile range and the retrieval
  dates.
- `osm.json.gz`: OpenStreetMap extract (retrieved 2026-11-23 11:01 UTC),
  reduced to geometry and the tags the importer reads; names, addresses
  and ids removed. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). This file and the generated
  map are derived databases and available under the ODbL 1.0.
- `dem.json.gz`: AHN4 DTM (Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland, PDOK
  coverage service `dtm_05m`, CC0 1.0), fetched as 30 GeoTIFF tiles of
  1 km at 1 m in RD New (EPSG:28992), reduced to 2 m cells in
  centimeters with the gaps under buildings and water filled
  (format `geo_import dem rd v1`).

Fetch again (raw downloads under `build/`, not in the repository): the
Overpass query of `site.json` once against `osm_endpoint`; the tiles with
`sources.ahn_url` for x0 = 185000 … 190000 and y0 = 427000 … 431000 in
steps of 1000 (x1 = x0 + 1000, y1 = y0 + 1000), then

    python3 -I tools/geo_import.py extract assets-src/geo/river_crossing/site.json \
        --osm build/geo116/raw/osm_overpass.json --ahn build/geo116/raw/ahn/*.tif
